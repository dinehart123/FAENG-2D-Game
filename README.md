Overview:
	My idea for a game is a platform fighter like smash bros or brawlhalla. But the characters are all animals and the theme follows. I won’t make too many characters, I’ll start with 1 or 2, as I don’t want to complicate things and keep my game within the scope of capability despite greater ambitions. The premise is to fight each other to knock each other off the stage and fall down or out of the arena (3 lives). The more damage you do to the opponent the more knockback they take. Each character will have unique attacks and thus unique animations. They will also have unique health, speed, weight, and damage. The more damage you take/deal the more your ultimate charges (not for certain on ultimate). You’ll be able to block, just like in smash bros, but I’ll also add a dodge just like in brawlhalla. There will be a start screen, choose your character screen (which will display stats), then the game screen, and game over screen. I want to be able to let people play against each other on the same computer.

Controls:
I will initially make the game keyboard and mouse because that’s what I’m used to on brawlhalla. But I easily see being able to implement a controller and just a keyboard. 
WASD - direction movement
Left & Right Click - for heavy and light attacks
Space - jump
Shift - Dodge
Alt - Shield
X - Ultimate

Art:
I haven’t yet decided if I want to use sprites or 3D models. They both have their pros and cons and I want to balance practicality with aesthetic appeal. I do know I want each attack to have custom animations, whether that’s just one separate sprite or a whole model of animations. If I am using 3D models I will for sure borrow assets from online. As for sprites I may borrow one, but then for the rest of the attack/movement animations I may have to create them as the sprites might have running/idle animations but I doubt they have fighting ones.

Audio:
I will combine audio assets from online and maybe make my own. Whether that be I make them by recording myself, or make them in garageband on my phone.
I have a cool idea I could use. I don’t know what game it is from but my coworker was telling me about a game that layered its music. The initial start screen has the background, then the character selection adds middle ground. Settings adds something else, etc.
Audio will play on attacks, and when the attacks land. When someone dies, when you click all the buttons, etc.


Concerns: 
Hitboxes/Collisions: Making sure the hitboxes are accurate during attack animations to ensure the game looks smooth, plays smooth, and is smooth. Hitboxes are clear, intuitive, and do not confuse players.

Physics: Making the movement feel right, acceleration, jumping, gravity, knockback, friction, air movement, dodging, etc.

Balancing: Making sure one character/attack/movement tech isn’t too broken or exploitable. Lots of testing, trial and error, guestimating, and potentially letting others try.

Animations: This may be time consuming. Could see myself running into a wall if I am using 3D models. Determining what animation to use (especially if I add idle).
